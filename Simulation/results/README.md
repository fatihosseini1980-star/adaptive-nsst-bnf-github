# Results files

- `final_raw_results_40rep.csv`: all 5 scenarios × 40 replications × 4 methods = 800 final rows.
- `final_summary_mean_sd_se_median_iqr.csv`: Monte Carlo mean, SD, SE, median, and IQR.
- `final_table_mean_sd.csv`: compact mean (SD) table.
- `development_calibration_raw.csv`: factor grid evaluated on 25 development fits.
- `development_calibration_summary.csv`: pooled calibration summary; minimum mean CRPS occurs at factor 1.20.
- `prior_sensitivity_raw_5rep.csv`: replication-level prior-sensitivity results for Scenarios 4 and 5.
- `prior_sensitivity_summary_5rep.csv`: summarized prior sensitivity.
- `convergence_summary.csv`: proposed-model optimization diagnostics by scenario.
- `surface_recovery_summary.csv`: quantitative alpha/delta/phi/correlation recovery diagnostics for Scenarios 4 and 5.
- `paired_differences_proposed_minus_benchmark.csv`: paired replication-level mean differences summarized against each benchmark.
